============================================================
  BIRTHLY — Liquid Glass Desktop Birthday Widget (v1.0)
============================================================

Thank you for installing Birthly!

QUICK START
-----------
- Move Widget:      Drag the top-left ":::" grip anywhere on your desktop.
- Add Birthdays:    Click "+ ADD" in the top-right (or click an empty day in Calendar view).
- Search & Filter:  Use the search bar (Ctrl+F), sort by Nearest / Furthest / Milestones
                    (special days lived like 222, 1,000, 10,000), or switch between LIST & CAL.
- Hero Spotlight:   Use "<" and ">" on the main card to preview any person's live countdown,
                    and click "COPY WISH" to copy a ready-to-send birthday message.
- Right-Click Menu: Right-click anywhere on Birthly for quick actions or to close the widget.

SETTINGS & CUSTOMIZATION
------------------------
Click "SETTINGS" (top-right) to customize:
  - GENERAL:     Alert highlight days, Age badge, Auto-Start, Widget Size, Refresh Rate
                 (locks to your monitor's native Hz), and Drag Refresh behavior.
  - APPEARANCE:  7 Glass Tints, 5 Fonts, Light Glass / Dark Mode, and a Live Preview where
                 you can drag the HERO block to rearrange the layout.
  - LAYERS:      Stay ABOVE or BELOW Desktop Icons, Rainmeter / Clock skins, or any .exe app.
  - OPTICS:      Balanced Liquid Glass, Crystal Clear Lens, or Soft Frosted Glass.
  - BACKUP:      Export or Import all people, settings & embedded photos in one ".birthly" file.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
------------------
  Ctrl + F          Focus search bar
  Esc               Close open menu, modal, search, editor, or settings
  Tab / Shift+Tab   Switch fields in Add/Edit Person
  Ctrl + V          Paste text into active field
  Ctrl + Backspace  Delete last word

WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED
-------------------------
Your birthdays, photos, and settings are saved safely per user in:
  %LocalAppData%\Birthly

UNINSTALLING BIRTHLY
--------------------
You can uninstall Birthly anytime in two ways:
  1. Windows Settings -> Apps -> Installed Apps -> Birthly -> Uninstall
  2. Double-click "Uninstall.exe" inside this installation folder (or via Start Menu).
